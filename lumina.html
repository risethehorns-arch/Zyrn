SYS.07 — CASE 01 / SHIPPED

A brand that survived being replaced.

Lumina is a private real-estate advisory in Amman. Zyrn built its brand kit and the site that carries it. Six weeks after launch the client asked for a different palette — every ground, every accent, every hairline — and it shipped in one commit, with no component rebuilt.

01

The brief

Who it sells to

Diplomats and foreign buyers arriving in Amman with a serious budget and a short timeline. They are not browsing. They have weeks, not seasons, and they need someone already holding the relationships.

What it sells

Villas, penthouses and standalone residences in Abdoun, Dabouq, Khalda and Um Uthaina — most of them never listed publicly, and represented on the condition that they stay that way.

What the site has to do

One job. Put a qualified enquiry into WhatsApp. Everything on the surface is measured against that, which is why the page argues for discretion before it shows a single price.

05

Still running

The kit outlived the design that introduced it.

That is the only durable test of a brand system. A kit that cannot absorb the client changing their mind is a set of pictures with a manual attached — and the bill for changing their mind arrives as a rebuild.

Lumina’s footer carries Zyrn’s wordmark, in Zyrn’s own type and Zyrn’s own violet, printed on their ground without a drop of amber on it. Ours carries theirs the same way. Neither mark was recoloured to fit the other, which is the shortest statement of the whole method we have.
