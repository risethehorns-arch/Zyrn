What it covers

Six modules. Every value is a number or a rule, never an adjective.

02a

Palette

Four values and a hairline. Each one with a stated job, so a fifth colour has to argue for itself.

02b

Type

Two families, four weights. A scale with sizes, tracking, and line-height, not a mood board.

02c

The mark

Construction, clear space, minimum size, and the one motion it is allowed to make.

02d

Motion law

Durations, easings, and what is forbidden. Motion is specified like spacing.

02e

Voice

How the firm states terms. Includes the list of things it does not say.

02f

Application

The kit applied across surface, deck, and document, so the rules survive contact with real work.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. Measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Three to four weeks. What the firm already signals, what it intends to, and where the two disagree.

P.02

Document

One quarter. The kit written to values, then applied across live surfaces to prove it holds.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. The kit is versioned; every exception is either absorbed into it or refused.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • CONSISTENCY New material is on-system without review.
  • DECISION COST Fewer arguments about taste, because the value is written down.
  • RECALL The mark survives being redrawn by someone outside the firm.
  • EXCEPTIONS Trend toward zero, or toward being absorbed into the system.
