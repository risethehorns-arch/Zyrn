Most firms manage headcount and call it talent strategy. Zyrn treats skills as infrastructure — mapped, versioned, and load-bearing — so the organization can be re-pointed without being rebuilt.

What it covers

Six modules. An engagement runs the ones the diagnostic calls for, never all six by default.

01a

Capability map

Every role reduced to the skills it actually consumes, and the ones it is only assumed to.

01b

Role redesign

Profiles rebuilt around outcomes and decisions rather than titles and tenure.

01c

Skills taxonomy

A living vocabulary the whole firm shares, versioned quarterly rather than authored once.

01d

Internal mobility

Adjacent-role pathways that move faster than job descriptions can be rewritten.

01e

Leader fluency

The layer that has to model the change before anyone beneath them will.

01f

Progression

Modular learning and recognised credentials attached to real work, not a catalogue.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. The engagement is measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Six to eight weeks. Skills inventory, role audit, and the distance between what the org chart claims and what the work actually requires.

P.02

Rebuild

One to two quarters. Profiles, taxonomy, and pathways installed and in daily use.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. Quarterly re-measure against the index. The taxonomy is versioned, never frozen.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • TIME-TO-FILL Internal candidates surface before an external search is opened.
  • MOBILITY Lateral moves stop being exceptions that require escalation.
  • ROLE DRIFT Descriptions stop diverging from the work within a quarter of being written.
  • LEADER LOAD Decisions stop escalating for want of a defined owner.
