Eleven people, three tiers, ten reporting lines. Every line on this chart is correct, everybody agrees it is correct, and the firm is still slow — because a reporting line says who is told, not who may decide.

What it covers

Six modules. Authority is mapped before any box is moved.

03a

Decision rights

Who decides what, at what threshold, and who only needs to be told. Written per decision, not per title.

03b

Incentives

What the current structure actually rewards, which is rarely what the strategy asks for.

03c

Operating model

How work crosses the firm: handoffs, queues, and the places load silently accumulates.

03d

Governance

The forums that decide, their cadence, and the ones that can be deleted outright.

03e

Accountability

A named owner per outcome. Shared ownership is treated as an unassigned outcome.

03f

Load

Where the structure is carrying more than it was designed for, measured before it is redesigned.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. Measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Four to six weeks. Decision mapping, incentive audit, and the escalation paths people actually use.

P.02

Restructure

One to two quarters. Rights reassigned, forums cut, and the operating model rewritten around them.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. Structure is reviewed against real decisions, quarter over quarter.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • ESCALATION Fewer decisions travel further than they need to.
  • CYCLE TIME The gap between a decision being needed and being made.
  • OWNERSHIP Every outcome resolves to one name.
  • FORUMS Count goes down; the remaining ones decide rather than update.

Shape the mandate

Where it hurts, how deep we may cut, the size of the firm. The chart redraws to your answers before anyone is asked to commit to anything.
