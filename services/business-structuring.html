What it covers

Six modules. Structure is diagnosed before anything is moved.

02a

Decision map

Every material decision, its real owner, and the distance between that and the stated owner.

02b

Accountability model

Ownership defined at the level where the information already sits.

02c

Incentive audit

What the firm rewards, read against what it says it wants.

02d

Operating cadence

The meeting, review, and escalation rhythm that carries the model.

02e

Governance spine

Thresholds, mandates, and the decisions deliberately left unmade.

02f

Interfaces

Contracts between functions, so handoffs stop being negotiations.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. The engagement is measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Six to eight weeks. A structural read taken from how decisions actually travel, not from the reporting lines.

P.02

Rebuild

One to two quarters. Decision rights, cadence, and interfaces redrawn and put under load.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. The model is measured against cycle time and rework, and adjusted quarterly.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • CYCLE TIME Decisions close in one pass instead of touring the organization.
  • ESCALATION Fewer decisions travel upward for want of a mandate.
  • OWNERSHIP Every material decision has one name against it.
  • REWORK Work stops being redone because two functions held the same assumption differently.
