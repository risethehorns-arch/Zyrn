This is the page as anyone arrives at it. Everything after this is what it is made of, and every one of those things is written down somewhere a developer can read.

What it covers

Six modules. Structure is settled before a pixel is drawn.

01a

Structure

What the firm is claiming, in what order, and which claims have to be provable. Architecture before layout.

01b

Design system

Tokens, type scale, spacing, motion law. Written down, so the surface can be extended without a redesign.

01c

Build

Static where static is correct, dynamic where it earns it. No framework the firm cannot maintain itself.

01d

Performance

Frame time and payload are acceptance criteria, not a pass at the end. A budget, not a hope.

01e

Accessibility

Contrast, motion preference, and keyboard paths are part of the build. Not an audit afterwards.

01f

Handover

The team ships a new page without asking how. That is the exit condition, and it is written into the engagement.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. Measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Four to six weeks. Surface audit, structure audit, and the distance between what the site claims and what it can show.

P.02

Build

One to two quarters. System documented, surface built, performance and accessibility held to budget.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. The surface is versioned like software, because that is what it is.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • QUALIFICATION Inbound arrives already understanding the terms.
  • FRAME BUDGET Held under target on the hardware the audience actually uses.
  • MAINTENANCE Changes ship from inside the firm, not from an agency queue.
  • DRIFT Claim and evidence stop separating between quarters.
