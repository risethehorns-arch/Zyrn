What it covers

Six modules, opening on the index. Nothing is deployed before it is scored.

03a

Readiness assessment

The index scored across strategy, workforce, workflow, governance, and data.

03b

Workflow re-engineering

Processes rebuilt around what the system does well, not retrofitted around what it cannot.

03c

Governance and risk

Thresholds, review, and the decisions that stay human by design.

03d

Data readiness

The inputs the system depends on, and the ones quietly unfit for it.

03e

Adoption program

Behavioural change sequenced by function and measured per cohort.

03f

Measurement

Value traced back to the workflow it came from, quarter over quarter.

How it runs

Three phases, the same three every line runs. The engagement is measured at each one.

P.01

Diagnose

Six to eight weeks. The readiness index is scored and the gap to the next level is made explicit.

P.02

Rebuild

One to two quarters. Workflows, governance, and the adoption program installed together.

P.03

Calibrate

Ongoing. The index is re-scored quarterly. If it does not move, the engagement has not worked.

What changes

Signals the diagnostic watches, and the direction they are expected to move.

  • INDEX The readiness score moves at least one level inside two quarters.
  • PILOT SURVIVAL Pilots reach production instead of quietly lapsing.
  • COVERAGE The share of core workflows the system genuinely carries.
  • SHADOW USE Unsanctioned tooling falls as the sanctioned path becomes the faster one.

Adoption,
not acquisition.
