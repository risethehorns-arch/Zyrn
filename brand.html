SYS.06 — BRAND SYSTEM / V.2.0

The instrument,
documented.

Every value on this page is load-bearing. Where a decision is not covered here, take the quieter option — that is the rule, not a fallback.

01 Doctrine

Four rules. They govern every ambiguous decision, and they outrank taste.

  1. Silence is the luxury

    80%+ of any viewport is empty or field. Density is what everyone else does.

  2. Metadata as ornament

    The only decoration permitted is real information — coordinates, indices, versions — set in mono.

  3. One Pulse per surface

    The accent appears exactly once per viewport. A second instance is a bug. On field surfaces it is emitted by the simulation and printed only on the mark — see 08.

  4. The shear belongs to the logo

    Never shear photography, headlines, or panels. One signature device, used once.

05b The glitch

A transmission artefact on a signal that is already sheared. It never un-shears anything: every layer is applied to a wrapper or to a throwaway clone, so the latched halves underneath are untouched by definition.

LayerBehaviour

A fixed interval reads as a metronome, which is the opposite of a glitch. Real artefacts cluster: mostly quiet, then two or three in quick succession. Each mark keeps its own randomised schedule, and marks that are off screen never fire.

06 Voice

Declarative, specific, and short. The brand does not sell; it states terms.

WRITE
  • “It installs the system and stays until it runs.”
  • “Every engagement opens on a number.”
  • “Four engagements a quarter. The constraint is deliberate.”
  • Real figures, indices, and dates as ornament.
NEVER
  • Exclamation marks, or any superlative.
  • “Cutting-edge”, “revolutionary”, “seamless”, “unlock”.
  • Claims of outcomes the index cannot show.
  • Faces, stock portraits, or team photography.

08 The field

SYS.07. A 90,000-point GPU simulation is the bed on every surface of the site. It is not footage, and there is no asset behind it — the page renders its own subject. Full spec in docs/spec-presence-field.md.

Ramp

Four stops, exposed as shader uniforms. The shipped ramp is vivid: it puts a green-teal at the cold end so the field travels through hue, not only through luminance. Pulse remains the anchor and the dominant stop. If the field reads flat, move the breakpoints, never the hues.

0.00 Teal
0.36 Cyan
0.72 Pulse
1.00 Vapor

EMITTED, NEVER PRINTED. THE TWO COLD STOPS EXIST ONLY AS LIGHT IN THE SIMULATION — NO DOM ELEMENT, ON ANY PAGE, MAY USE THEM AS INK.

The strict in-palette ramp — Steel #767E8C → Pulse → Pulse-lifted #A99BFF → Vapor — is still available and is one word per page: initField({ ramp: 'strict' }). It reads as a luminance ramp rather than a hue ramp: quieter, and closer to the original doctrine.

Each surface runs its own program over these four states, and its own tilt and spin, so moving between pages reads as one instrument changing channels. The landing page runs all four across SYS.01–05; each service page runs two.

Departures

The field required three amendments to the rules above. They are in force on every surface except this one.

  • 03 — Additive light is permitted

    “No glows, no noise” exists to stop decorative glow being sprinkled on a flat page. Where emitted light is the entire medium, there is no flat page to protect.

  • 04 — The field is the Pulse

    Because the simulation carries Pulse continuously, no button, rule or hover state prints the accent — on a field surface it is light, not ink. The mark is the exception. The seam is specified in 03 as 2px Pulse with a 12px overhang, and it stays exactly that: the signature is the one place the accent is still printed. It draws on --pulse-mark, a token that exists only for the wordmark, the monogram and the glitch slice, because --pulse itself is remapped to Vapor everywhere else.

  • 05 — The count is read, not typed

    The hero states its particle count as live metadata. If the performance governor steps the field down, the number changes with it. Doctrine rule two — the only decoration is real information — taken to its conclusion.
